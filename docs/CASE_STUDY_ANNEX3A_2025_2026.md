# First verified historical version contrast: Cabinet Resolution 214, Annex 3a, Clause 3

Date logged: 2026-10-27

## Official sources
- [Historical text at 26 November 2025](https://lex.uz/uz/docs/-6479366?ONDATE=26.11.2025)
- [Amended text at 3 July 2026](https://lex.uz/uz/docs/-6479366?ONDATE=03.07.2026)
- [Amending Cabinet Resolution No. 353, adopted 30 June 2026 and effective 3 July 2026](https://lex.uz/docs/-8306415?ONDATE=03.07.2026)

**Scope:** specialized foreign-language boarding schools under **Annex 3a** of Resolution 214, *not* presidential schools and not the Annex 3 creative-school committee.

## Human-provided and source-corroborated clauses
**2025 snapshot:** clause 3 sets annual admissions at two classes with 24 students each (48 total). It does not spell out a separate 1–4 versus 5–11 band in that clause.

**2026 amended edition:** clause 3 specifies two classes with 12 students each for grades 1–4, and two classes with 24 students each for grades 5–11.

The new wording cannot simply be interpreted as "72 students in one cohort": grade groups and implementation scope must not be silently combined.

## Research contribution and limitations
The same clause identifier has different relevant wording in two historical editions, allowing a genuine temporal retrieval contrast at **2026-07-02** versus **2026-07-03**. The old edition's original starting validity date has NOT been independently established: the prototype dataset clips its start to the **2025-11-26 observed snapshot**. Do not misrepresent this as a change enacted on 26 November 2025.

The current candidate queries were written for engineering checks and must undergo independent human review before becoming research gold labels. There are only **4 independent question groups / 12 script variants** in this added case, and just two highly related passages. It is not evidence of generalization or model superiority.
